How it works

      

        Android builds its Material You (Monet) palette from a single seed color. Normally that
        seed comes from your wallpaper, or from a short list of preset "basic colors". The same
        setting the Wallpaper & style app writes, theme_customization_overlay_packages,
        accepts any color. This page writes it over ADB, directly from Chrome using WebUSB.

FAQ

      

        Does it need root or an app on the phone?
        
No. ADB's shell user is allowed to change this setting, so nothing gets installed on the phone.

      

      

        Which phones work?
        
Tested on a Pixel 8 Pro running Android 17. Any Pixel or near-stock Android 12+ phone with Material You should work, but other phones haven't been tested.

      

      

        Will the color stay after I unplug or reboot?
        
Yes. It's a normal system setting. Picking colors in Wallpaper & style again will replace it; just re-apply here.

      

      

        Can I set secondary and tertiary colors separately?
        
Not with this setting: Android derives them from the seed. The style (Vibrant, Expressive, Rainbow…) controls how.

      

      

        How do I undo it?
        
Click "Back to wallpaper colors", or pick any color in Wallpaper & style on the phone.

      

      

        Is my data sent anywhere?
        
No. Everything runs locally in your browser; the page talks only to your phone over USB.
